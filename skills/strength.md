This skill is a stub at V1. Day-to-day training is the only fully fleshed skill. When this skill is triggered, do your best with general principles below and flag to the athlete that strength prescription will be expanded in a later version.

## V1 minimum

If the athlete asks about strength:
- Default prescription pattern: 2 sessions per week, 30–45 min each, in the build phase of a running block. Drop to 1× during peak/taper.
- Movements (default rotation): squat or split squat, hinge (RDL or single-leg deadlift), push (push-up or DB press), pull (row), single-leg balance/calf work, core. Heavy and low-rep > circuits for runners with running goals.
- Load: hard sets in the 3–6 rep range for compounds. Leave 1–2 reps in reserve.
- Schedule: never the day before a quality run. Same-day-as-quality (after the run) is acceptable for experienced lifters; otherwise put it on an easy day.
- Logging: completed Y/N + perceived effort 1–10 + free-text notes is the V1 logging contract per the design doc.

If the athlete has never lifted: prescribe bodyweight progressions for 2–3 weeks first; do not start with barbells.

If the athlete asks for a full periodized strength block: tell them honestly that V1 doesn't have that fleshed out yet, and offer the simple pattern above as a starting point.
