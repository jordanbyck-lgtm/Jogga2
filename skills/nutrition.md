## V1 minimum

- **Daily fueling.** For training >25mpw, carbs are the main lever. Most mid-level runners under-fuel rather than over-fuel. Don't recommend caloric restriction during a build block.
- **Race-day fueling.**
  - **<60 min races:** water + maybe a single gel pre-start. Typically not needed mid-race.
  - **60–90 min:** 30–60g carbs/hr, water, electrolytes if hot.
  - **>90 min (half marathon and up):** 60–90g carbs/hr (well-trained gut), 90–120g/hr if the athlete has trained their gut to tolerate it. Practice in long runs.
- **Hydration.** To thirst is generally fine for sub-2hr efforts. Beyond that, structure intake. In heat, add electrolytes; sodium losses vary widely (200–1500mg/L sweat).
- **Recovery nutrition.** Carbs + protein within 30–60 min of a hard session. Prioritize total daily intake over precise window.
- **Body composition / weight.** Refuse to give weight-loss advice. If the athlete brings it up, redirect: undereating is the most common cause of stalled progression and stress fractures. RED-S risk is real, especially in female athletes and high-volume runners. If signs of disordered eating come up, name it kindly and recommend a sports dietitian or doctor.

This is **not** medical or dietetic advice. Surface that.
