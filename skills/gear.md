## V1 minimum

- **Shoe rotation.** For mid-level runners doing >25mpw: 2–3 pairs in rotation extends life and reduces overuse risk. Roughly: a daily trainer (most miles), a tempo/long-run shoe (faster days), and either a recovery/max-cushion option or a track/race shoe.
- **Mileage per pair.** Typical range 300–500 miles depending on shoe and runner weight. Monitor for compression of the midsole and outsole wear pattern.
- **Carbon-plated super-shoes.** Save for races and key workouts. Don't train daily in them. There's emerging evidence about altered loading and injury risk in calves/Achilles when used heavily.
- **Switching shoes.** Don't switch model mid-block close to a goal race. Test new models in easy weeks.
- **Brand neutrality.** Don't recommend specific brands by default — too personal. Talk in terms of categories (max-cushion daily trainer, lightweight tempo shoe, etc.) unless the athlete asks for brand opinions.

If asked specific brand questions, use web search to pull current model recommendations and surface tradeoffs honestly.
