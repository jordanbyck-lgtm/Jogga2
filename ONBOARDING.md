# Onboarding — first session

The coach walks the athlete through these one at a time. Every question is skippable. Free-text answers are encouraged. The coach does not ask follow-ups during onboarding — clarifying questions come later.

After the last question, the coach generates the first plan and asks for a gut-check.

---

## 1. Identity

What's your name and how should I refer to you? Anything you want me to know about you up front — age, where you live, what you do — is welcome but optional.

→ writes to `harness/ATHLETE.md`

---

## 2. Running history

How long have you been running, and what does your running history look like? Past PRs, race distances you've done, any years off, biggest training cycles you've completed. The more context the better.

→ writes to `harness/ATHLETE.md`

---

## 3. Current weekly volume

Roughly how much are you running per week right now (miles or km) and over how many days? If it's been variable lately, give me a recent typical week.

→ writes to `harness/CURRENT_STATE.md`

---

## 4. Paces and zones

What paces do you run by feel — easy, tempo, threshold, intervals? If you have any of: a recent race time, lab/treadmill threshold test, VDOT, FTP-equivalent, HR zones — give me what you have. Skipping is fine; I'll estimate from a recent run.

→ writes to `harness/CURRENT_STATE.md`

---

## 5. Goals

What are you training for? A specific race (with date), a fitness goal, multiple races, or just generally want to get faster / stay healthy. If there's a primary goal, tell me about it. Secondary goals welcome.

→ writes to `harness/GOALS.md`

---

## 6. Weekly availability

Which days can you run, roughly how long each day, and which days are protected (work, family, mandatory rest)? If your schedule changes week to week, describe the variation.

→ writes to `harness/CONSTRAINTS.md`

---

## 7. Injury history

Any current injuries or pain? Anything chronic? Past major injuries (stress fractures, surgery, anything that pulled you out for >2 weeks)? Anything you're nervous about. Don't hold back — this is the most important question for keeping you healthy.

→ writes to `harness/CONSTRAINTS.md`

---

## 8. Biomechanics and physiology

Anything notable: flat feet, high arches, leg length discrepancy, ankle mobility issues, weak glutes, recurring tightness somewhere, asymmetries you've noticed, prior PT diagnoses. Skip if nothing comes to mind.

→ writes to `harness/PHYSIOLOGY.md`

---

## 9. What you like and hate

In past training: what worked, what you liked, what you hated, what you bailed on. Surface preference (road, trail, track), group vs solo, music vs no music, mornings vs evenings, treadmill tolerance, weather you'll and won't run in.

→ writes to `harness/PREFERENCES.md`

---

## 10. Coach persona

Three options:
- **Coach Jordan** — plain, direct, technical (recommended default)
- **Coach Syd** — talks like they don't actually like running and loves sleeping in
- **Coach Christine** — sassy
- Or "I don't know" — defaults to Jordan

This is tone only. The recommendations are identical across personas.

→ writes to `harness/PREFERENCES.md`

---

## After question 10

The coach:
1. Generates the first plan: `plan/OVERVIEW.md` + the next two `plan/weeks/week_*.md` files, locked.
2. Asks for a gut-check on the plan.
3. Calls out specific things that might be off ("this is a big jump in mileage from what you described — does that feel right?").
