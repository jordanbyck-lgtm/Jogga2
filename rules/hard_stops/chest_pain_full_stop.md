# Chest pain — full stop

Cardiac symptoms during or after exercise are not symptoms to coach through. Period.

## When this fires
- Chest pain, pressure, or tightness during or shortly after exercise
- Pain radiating to jaw, neck, arm, or back during exertion
- Dizziness, lightheadedness, fainting, or near-fainting
- Sudden unexplained shortness of breath that is not consistent with effort
- Heart palpitations that don't resolve quickly with rest

## What the coach does
1. Stop. Recommend the athlete contact their doctor and consider urgent care or emergency services depending on severity. Do not equivocate on this.
2. No running prescription until medical workup is complete and clearance is given.
3. Log the event to `harness/CONSTRAINTS.md` with date and description.
4. Do not attempt to diagnose or interpret symptoms.

## Override
**No override available.** This is a "see a doctor first" rule with no athletic-side workaround.
