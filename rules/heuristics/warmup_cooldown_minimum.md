# Warmup and cooldown minimums

## Why
Cold tissue at race-pace effort is a recipe for injury. The literature isn't precise on minimums, but coaching consensus is reliable: at least 10 minutes of easy aerobic warmup before threshold-or-faster work, more for shorter and faster intervals (R-pace and faster benefits from 15–20 min warmup with strides).

## What the coach does
- Include explicit warmup and cooldown durations in every prescribed quality session.
- Default: 10 min easy + drills/strides for interval/threshold. 15–20 min for VO2/repetition work.
- Cooldown: 5 min minimum, longer is better. 10 min for hard sessions.
- Include 4–6 strides in warmup before VO2 or repetition work.

## When override is reasonable
- Tempo runs that build gradually from easy can compress the warmup since the pace ramps naturally.
- Hill reps where the run to the hill serves as warmup (specify clearly in the prescription).
