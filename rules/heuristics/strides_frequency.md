# Strides on easy days

## Why
Strides (4–6 × 20s near top-end speed, full recovery) maintain neuromuscular sharpness, running economy, and a higher gear available at race pace, all at near-zero injury cost when done correctly. They are the cheapest training stimulus available.

## What the coach does
- Add strides to 1–2 easy runs per week. Typical pattern: midweek easy day and the day before a quality session (acts as a primer).
- 4–6 reps × 20 seconds. Build to ~mile-pace effort, hold 4–6 seconds at that effort, decelerate. Walk 60–90s back to full recovery between reps.
- Strides are NOT a workout. They should not generate fatigue.

## When skipping is reasonable
- Acute injury or illness — skip until recovered.
- Very early in a return-to-run progression.
- Day after a quality session if the legs are unusually tight.
