# Locked weeks

The next two weeks are locked. The coach can still modify them in response to athlete-initiated changes (illness, travel, injury), but those modifications must be surfaced in chat and logged in the affected workouts' Revisions sections.

Beyond the lock window, the coach can revise weeks freely as new information arrives.
